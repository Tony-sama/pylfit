# Authors

Tony Ribeiro wrote pylfit's code, both 0.x and 1.0, and maintains it
([Tony-sama](https://github.com/Tony-sama)).

The users who shaped what pylfit does, the people who developed the
Learning From Interpretation Transitions (LFIT) framework it
implements, and the work that applies LFIT are credited on the
[Credits](https://github.com/Tony-sama/pylfit/blob/next/docs/credits.md)
page.

See also the list of
[contributors](https://github.com/Tony-sama/pylfit/graphs/contributors).
