## Where to start

- To use pylfit, start with [Getting started](getting-started.md),
  then [Prepare your data](how-to/prepare-data.md). The
  [how-to guides](how-to/index.md) cover specific tasks.
- To work through a whole problem, follow a
  [tutorial](tutorials/index.md): heart disease from clinical data, or
  a gene regulatory network from expression time series.
- To understand how it works, read
  [About LFIT rules](explanation/about-rules.md) and the other
  [explanations](explanation/index.md).
- To decide whether pylfit fits your problem, read
  [Why pylfit, and when not to use it](explanation/why-pylfit.md).
- To see runnable scripts and what they print, browse the
  [examples](auto_examples/index.rst).
- To look something up, use the [API reference](api/index.md).
- To cite pylfit in published work, see [Citing pylfit](citing.md).
- To contribute, see [Contributing](contributing/index.md).
